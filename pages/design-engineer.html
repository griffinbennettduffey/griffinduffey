Devinci House

Designer & developer

Devinci House is a contemporary art gallery in downtown Greenville, SC. We wanted the feel of the site to bring together the young, organic, analog feel to the ethos behind the gallery while still making clear that Devinci House means business and is up there with the best of the high end galleries. We felt inspired by Windows 98 CSS styles and icons, and felt it complimented the VHS video background from a pop-up before the gallery had officially launched. We wanted to welcome the site visitor with Y2K energy, while keeping internal pages clean and minimalist.

Throw

Designer & developer

Throw was a small site design & development service that I was given the lead on from Meridian. We wanted to offer a service that focuses on a hand-thrown site feel. So much of the web is made by and for robots, while still so many humans want to make websites, brands, and businesses that are people-first. Service has ended for this but you can still see the work.

Site was featured on One Page Love.
